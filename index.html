About FocusFlow

FocusFlow is a browser-based sound therapy app that generates all audio in real-time using the Web Audio API — no downloads, no streaming, no latency. Every tone, beat and soundscape is synthesized live on your device.

Our mission is to make evidence-informed sound therapy accessible to everyone — students, professionals, people managing anxiety, insomnia, stress or simply anyone who wants a calmer, more focused mind.

🎧
Use Headphones
Binaural beats only work with headphones. Each ear receives a slightly different frequency, and your brain creates the beat.
🔬
Science-Backed
Based on research in psychoacoustics, brainwave entrainment, and music therapy. Alpha and theta waves have measurable calming effects.
🌐
100% Browser
No server, no login, no tracking. Everything runs locally in your browser using the Web Audio API.
🔀
Layer Freely
Combine any sounds — binaural + nature + tone — to create your perfect personal soundscape with individual volume controls.
